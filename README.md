# Digital Crimes — reel production

Production files and temporary video hosting for the Instagram channel
[@digital_crimes](https://www.instagram.com/digital_crimes/): cyber law and cyber forensics, explained.

- `engine/` — the animation engine and original music generator used for every reel
- `content/calendar.md` — the episode schedule
- `episodes/` — one folder per reel (script, sources, caption, cover); videos are removed after posting

All content is legal awareness only and not legal advice. Music and visuals are original.
