# Hashtags (owner's instruction, 26 Nov 2026)

Instagram allows at most **5 hashtags** on a reel, counting the caption and comments together. Hashtags file a
reel under a topic and in Instagram search. They do not add reach on their own. Every reel therefore mixes the
**most-used** cyber tags (large audience) with one **exact** tag (fewer posts, but searched by exactly the right
people). Every tag must be true to the reel. Hashtags go only on the last line of `caption.txt`, never in a comment.

## The five slots, in this order

1. **Big tag**: one of the most-used cyber tags, matched to the topic:
   - #CyberSecurity (about 5M posts): technology, forensics, corporate, data breach
   - #CyberCrime: crime, investigation, police procedure, law
   - #Scam (about 550K) or #Fraud (about 450K+): scam and fraud reels
   - #Privacy (about 970K): tracking, metadata, data, privacy reels
2. **Awareness tag** (mid-size):
   - #CyberSecurityAwareness (about 500K): default
   - #ScamAlert (about 370K): scam reels
   - #Phishing (about 125K): link, SMS, APK and email-trap reels only
   - #DataProtection (about 200K+): data, DPDP, leak reels
   - #InfoSec (about 370K+): corporate reels
3. **Exact tag**: the reel's own subject, in the words people search:
   #UPIFraud #DigitalArrest #OnlineFraud #CyberFraud #DigitalForensics #CyberLaw #DigitalEvidence #SIMSwap
   #Deepfake #AccountFreeze #MoneyMule #CEOFraud #InvestmentScam #JobScam #FakeLoanApp #WhatsAppScam
   #IdentityTheft #Ransomware #MobileTracking.
   Never a made-up tag nobody searches (#HashValue, #BossScam, #WhatsAppCall).
4. **Audience tag** (the script's primary audience):
   - Law and investigation learners: #LawStudent (about 1M) or #LLB (about 210K)
   - Everyday UPI users and families: #CyberSafety or #OnlineSafety
   - Parents and senior citizens: #SeniorCitizens or #CyberSafety
   - Students and job seekers: #CollegeStudents or #OnlineSafety
   - Shopkeepers, freelancers, small businesses: #SmallBusiness or #DigitalPayments
   - Corporate decision-makers: #InfoSec or #DataProtection (whichever slot 2 didn't use)
5. **Brand**: #DigitalCrimes, always last.

Post counts are approximate figures from third-party hashtag trackers (Nov 2026 searches; some data is older).
If two slots would give the same tag, take the other option.

## Rules
- Exactly 5, all different, CamelCase (#UPIFraud), English letters only, also on Hindi-voice days.
- Rotate: slots 1–4 are never the identical set on two reels in a row.
- Never use:
  - empty reach or spam tags: #reels #reelsinstagram #explore #explorepage #viral #trending #fyp #foryou
    #instagood #bhfyp (Instagram says tags like these don't help and can hurt a reel);
  - hacking tags: #hacker #hackers #hacking #hack #ethicalhacking #kalilinux (crowded with hacking-for-hire spam);
  - anything that advertises legal services (BCI Rule 36): #lawyer #advocate #cyberlawyer #lawfirm
    #legaladvice #legalhelp #freelegaladvice #bestlawyer, or any city + lawyer tag;
  - names of real people, victims, accused persons, companies, banks or payment apps (#Paytm, #PhonePe…),
    except the platform the scam runs on when that is the topic (#WhatsAppScam);
  - government handles, schemes or campaign tags (for example #CyberDost), so nothing suggests an official link;
  - sexual-offence words; for those topics use #OnlineSafety or #CyberSafety;
  - fear or clickbait tags (#shocking #scary #mustwatch).
- The review email shows the hashtag line; the pre-post checklist checks it against this file.
