No signal? GPS still knows where you are.
⚖️ For educational purposes only. Not legal advice.

At least 24 GPS satellites keep broadcasting their position and the exact time; your phone turns the signal delays from at least 4 of them into a location, even with no mobile network. The satellites never learn where you are, but your phone and apps may store it: investigators can require such records under Section 94 BNSS, and in court they need a Section 63 BSA certificate. In July 2024 the Supreme Court held that a bail condition cannot let police constantly track an accused (Article 21).

Check which apps can see your location: Settings → Location → App permissions.
