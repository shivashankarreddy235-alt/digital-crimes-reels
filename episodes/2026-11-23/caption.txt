"You are under digital arrest." No, you're not.

There is no "digital arrest" in Indian law. Under Section 43(1) of the BNSS, an arrest needs the police to actually touch or confine you. A video call can't do that. The caller is the one committing crimes: impersonating a public servant, cheating, extortion and forgery under the BNS, and Section 66D of the IT Act.

Threatened on a call? Disconnect, call 1930, report at cybercrime.gov.in, and report the number on Sanchar Saathi (Chakshu).
