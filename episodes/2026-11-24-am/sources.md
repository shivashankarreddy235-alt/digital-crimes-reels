# EP03 sources (opened 23 Nov 2026)

## The law
- **BNSS 2023 s.43(1)** "Arrest how made": the arresting officer "shall actually touch or confine the body of the person to be
  arrested, unless there be a submission to the custody by word or action" (old law: CrPC s.46):
  https://www.writinglaw.com/section-43-bnss/ ; earlier check: https://leadindia.law/bharatiya-nagarik-suraksha-sanhita/bnss-section-43-arrest-how-made
- **BNS s.204** Personating a public servant ("pretends to hold any particular office as a public servant"); bare text: 6 months
  to 3 years and fine (the page's own commentary says "two years"; bare text used, punishment not shown in the reel):
  https://www.apnilaw.com/bare-act/bns/section-204-bharatiya-nyaya-sanhita-bns-personating-a-public-servant
- **BNS s.319** Cheating by personation, up to 5 years: https://devgan.in/bns/section/319/
- **BNS s.318(4)** Cheating and dishonestly inducing delivery of property, up to 7 years + fine: https://devgan.in/bns/index.php?a=10&q=318
- **BNS s.336** Forgery incl. "false electronic record"; s.336(3) forgery for cheating, up to 7 years + fine:
  https://devgan.in/bns/index.php?a=10&q=336
- **BNS s.308** Extortion: puts a person in fear of injury and dishonestly induces delivery of property; s.308(2) up to 7 years;
  illustration covers threats sent through an electronic device:
  https://www.advocatekhoj.com/library/bareacts/bharatiyanyayasanhita/308.php
- **IT Act 2000 s.66D** Cheating by personation by means of a communication device or computer resource: up to 3 years + fine up
  to ₹1 lakh: https://lawx.in/acts/section/17/224/2231
- Offences used in digital-arrest cases (BNS 204, 318, 319, 336, 308; IT Act 66C/66D) — legal commentary:
  https://disputeresolution.cyrilamarchandblogs.com/2025/02/from-clicks-to-cuffs-understanding-digital-arrest-in-the-indian-legal-landscapes/ ;
  https://www.scconline.com/blog/post/2025/11/05/understanding-cyber-frauds-digital-arrests-protection-tips-law-made-easy-scc-times/
- **BNSS s.94(1)**: a Court or the officer in charge of a police station can require "production of any document, electronic
  communication, including communication devices" likely to contain digital evidence:
  https://www.advocatekhoj.com/library/bareacts/bharatiyanagarik2023/94.php ; use for CDRs/platform data (EP01 check):
  https://www.scconline.com/blog/post/2025/11/25/production-of-call-details-right-to-privacy-criminal-trial-miscarriage-of-justice-ph-hc/

## How callers are traced
- Money trail: CFCFRMS (1930) alerts the bank's nodal officer; funds still there are put on hold, otherwise the ticket moves to
  the next bank in the chain: https://moneylife.in/article/fraud-alert-use-golden-hour-to-report-scam-and-increase-your-chances-to-get-money-back/74091.html
- Layers of mule accounts, traced through statements and KYC; CDR, subscriber records (CAF) and IPDR analysed in a
  digital-arrest probe (general method only, no names): https://www.freepressjournal.in/mumbai/maharashtra-cyber-police-arrests-2-more-in-58-crore-digital-arrest-scam-probe-tracks-20000-mule-accounts-across-13-layers ;
  money moved through 9 layers / 16 banks in two days; speed of reporting matters: https://scroll.in/article/1078480/my-father-lost-rs-10-lakh-in-a-digital-arrest-scam-heres-how-he-got-some-money-back
- CDR / IMEI / IPDR basics (EP01 checks): https://telanganatoday.com/how-ipdr-is-transforming-cybercrime-investigations-in-india
- Spoofed calls: DoT's International Incoming Spoofed Calls Prevention System launched 22 Oct 2024; ~1.35 crore spoofed calls
  blocked in the first 24 hours; DoT says spoofed calls were used in fake digital arrests:
  https://www.newsonair.gov.in/international-incoming-spoofed-calls-prevention-system-launched ;
  https://pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2095917 ; https://pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2080641
- Chakshu (Sanchar Saathi): report suspected fraud calls/SMS/WhatsApp, incl. impersonation of government officials; money
  already lost → 1930 / cybercrime.gov.in: https://sancharsaathi.gov.in/sfc/
