Fraud money moves at once from a "Layer 1" mule account into more accounts, then out as ATM cash or crypto. A 1930 complaint sends a ticket bank to bank, and money still in an account can be held: over ₹11,158 crore has been saved this way (MHA, till 30 June 2026). Cheating is punishable under BNS Section 318(4); knowingly receiving or keeping such money, under Section 317(2). Under BNSS Section 107, a Magistrate can attach proceeds of crime and order them returned to victims.

Lost money? Call 1930 at once or report on cybercrime.gov.in, then call your bank. Never rent or sell your bank account.
