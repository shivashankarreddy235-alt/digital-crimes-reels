Your phone is never really alone.

Every call, SMS and data session is logged with the tower and sector that served it: the Call Detail Record (CDR). Courts and police can require operators to produce these records under Section 94 of the BNSS, and under the DoT licence operators must keep them for at least two years. In court, a CDR needs a certificate under Section 63 of the Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam.

The same records can also prove an innocent person was somewhere else.
