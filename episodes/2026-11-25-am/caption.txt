Fraud money paid to a shop can be traced through a 1930 complaint, and the bank may freeze the account. Freeze notices often cite BNSS Section 106; a seizure must be reported to the Magistrate. High Courts have said the hold should ordinarily stay on the disputed amount, not the whole account (MP HC, Jul 2026; Allahabad HC, Aug 2026). Under BNS Section 317(2), receiving such money is an offence only if done dishonestly, knowing or having reason to believe it was stolen.

Keep a bill and the UPI reference for every sale. If frozen, ask your bank in writing for the complaint details and raise a grievance.
