# EP05 — A stranger sent you money, and now your whole account is frozen

**Series:** KNOW YOUR RIGHTS (green badge) · **Primary audience:** shopkeepers, freelancers and anyone who takes UPI payments
(QR and soundbox merchants, small traders); second audience: law learners (freeze-and-release process).
Slot: 2026-11-25 am (7:00 AM IST) · on-screen text English · voice **Hindi** (AI voice: the ElevenLabs voice set in the owner's
account) · section numbers and 1930 written as English words so they are spoken in English (owner's EP03 rule).
Scenario: **dramatised** — "Rekha, 36, Nashik", her clothes shop, the ₹5,000 sale and the ₹3,40,000 balance are invented and
illustrative; all account numbers and UPI IDs masked. Not any client or case. Built from the reported pattern (Telangana
Today, Jun 2026; the AP HC's ₹1,000 UPI-sale case).
Why today: High Courts keep stepping in on bank freezes after cyber complaints (Delhi HC 20 Nov; Allahabad HC 12 Nov) — see trends.md.

series asks) · 05 DO THIS NOW (12). Chapter cards open scenes 2, 4, 5, 7 and 12 (wipe + sting).
