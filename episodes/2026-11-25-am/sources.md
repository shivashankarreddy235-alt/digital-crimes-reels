## The law

- **BNSS 2023 s.106(1) and (3).** VERIFIED (India Code bare text, opened by the EP04 build run on 24 Nov 2026).
  - s.106(1): "Any police officer may seize any property which may be alleged or suspected to have been stolen, or which
    may be found under circumstances which create suspicion of the commission of any offence."
  - s.106(3): the officer "shall forthwith report the seizure to the Magistrate having jurisdiction".
  - Source: https://indiacode.nic.in/bitstream/123456789/20099/3/A2023-46.pdf · Old law: CrPC s.102.
  - Use: freeze notices to banks cite s.106 — MP HC order (notice "under Section 106 BNSS", para 2); Allahabad HC (lien under
    s.106); Delhi HC 20 Nov (SOP clause 10.2 ties seizure directions to s.106). Reel says "often", not "always".
  - Not claimed: whether a whole-account debit freeze is lawful under s.106 — High Courts are split (EP04 sources.md).

- **BNS 2023 s.317(2).** VERIFIED (India Code BNS PDF, opened by the EP04 run on 24 Nov 2026).
  - "Whoever dishonestly receives or retains any stolen property, knowing or having reason to believe the same to be stolen
    property, shall be punished with imprisonment … up to three years, or with fine, or with both."
  - s.317(1): property transferred by cheating is "stolen property".
  - Source: https://www.indiacode.nic.in/bitstream/123456789/20062/1/a202345.pdf · Old law: IPC s.411.
  - Reel wording: an honest sale is "not a crime by itself"; the offence needs "knowing or having reason to believe".

## Courts on freezing the whole account (primary orders opened this run)

- **Madhya Pradesh High Court, WP 29117 of 2026, order of 27 Jul 2026 (Justice Himanshu Joshi).** VERIFIED.
  - Order PDF: https://www.livelaw.in/pdf_upload/2026/07/28/archana-v-state-of-madhya-pradesh-689886.pdf
  - Disputed transaction Rs 980; balance "more than Rs. 2,51,72,192.57" (paras 3, 18).
  - Para 13: "Freezing of an entire account containing legitimate funds merely because a negligible amount is suspected …
    must satisfy the test of proportionality".
  - Para 15(I): "Freezing of the entire bank account shall be resorted to only in exceptional circumstances", for reasons
    recorded in writing; ordinarily a lien or debit freeze "only to the extent of the suspected amount" (15(IV)).
  - Para 15(V): bank to inform the account holder of the freeze (unless the law bars it or it would seriously prejudice the probe).
  - Para 17: bank uploads the grievance within 7 days, IO decides within 15 days; these are "outer limits".
  - Refers to an MHA SOP dated 10.04.2026. Guidelines apply in MP "until appropriate statutory rules or executive instructions are framed".
  - Report: LiveLaw, 28 Jul 2026: https://www.livelaw.in/amp/high-court/madhya-pradesh-high-court/freezing-bank-accounts-guidelines-cybercrime-complaints-grievance-redressal-543201
  - Party name not shown in the reel (private individual).

- **Allahabad High Court (Lucknow), WRIT-C 6602 of 2026, order of 6 Aug 2026 (Shekhar B. Saraf and Abdhesh Kumar Chaudhary, JJ.).** VERIFIED.
  - Order PDF: https://www.livelaw.in/pdf_upload/2026/08/14/ritesh-yadav-vs-reserve-bank-of-india-new-delhi-thru-its-governor-and-6-others-2026-livelaw-ab-578-allahabad-high-court-693898.pdf
  - Disputed amount ₹36,000. Para 15: "continuation of a blanket debit freeze over the entire bank accounts of the petitioner",
    to the extent it operates beyond ₹36,000, "cannot be sustained in its present form"; de-freeze "after marking a lien of ₹36,000/-".
  - Para 18(i): "the restraint, if any, shall remain confined to the said amount".
  - Quotes the MHA SOP para 10.2(b): grievance submitted by the bank "not beyond 07 calendar days"; 10.2(d): IO decides
    "within 15 calendar days". Escalation: District, then State grievance officer (LiveLaw report, 14 Aug 2026).
  - Report: https://www.livelaw.in/high-court/allahabad-high-court/cyber-fraud-allahabad-hc-bank-lien-disputed-amount-mha-sop-545790
  - The court also said it "is conscious of the increasing menace of cyber financial fraud" and kept the power to restrain
    accounts (paras 9, 20). Reel: "Each ruling turns on its own facts."

- **Andhra Pradesh High Court, WP 969 of 2026, order of 22 Jun 2026 (Justice Ravi Cheemalapati).** VERIFIED.
  - Order PDF: https://www.livelaw.in/pdf_upload/2026/07/06/1-2-684649.pdf
  - A licensed trader's account (balance Rs 8,26,633) frozen on a cyber cell's instruction after a Rs 1,000 transaction
    (19.08.2025) by a customer later accused in a cheating case (para 6, as recited).
  - Para 6: "the vendor cannot verify the credentials of such individual"; "the freezing the petitioner's account is illegal"
    (also: no notice, no due process, involvement not verified). Para 7: bank directed to defreeze.
  - Short, uncontested order (respondents did not appear). **The order contains the real account number: never shown.**
  - Report: LiveLaw, 6 Jul 2026: https://www.livelaw.in/amp/high-court/andhra-pradesh-high-court/andhra-pradesh-high-court-vendors-verify-upi-customer-antecedents-540137

- **Kerala HC, 28 Oct 2026; Allahabad HC (Lucknow), 12 Nov 2026; Delhi HC, 20 Nov 2026.** Background (trends.md). Not on screen.

## MHA SOP for NCRP-CFCFRMS (2026)

- **Lien limited to the disputed sum; whole-account freeze the exception.** PARTLY (SOP text not opened).
  - LiveLaw opinion (Vikram Singh Kushwaha, 11 Jun 2026, "views are personal"): the SOP "requires that, where a lien is to be
    placed, it be limited to the disputed sum": https://www.livelaw.in/articles/cfcfrms-reading-mha-new-account-freeze-sop-537503
  - Business Standard, 15 Jan 2026 (MHA "approved" the SOP): https://www.business-standard.com/india-news/mha-sop-cyber-fraud-refunds-below-50000-no-court-order-126011500706_1.html
  - The reel puts the "amount, not account" principle on the **courts'** orders (verified above), not on the SOP.
- **Grievance route (7 days / 15 days, then District and State officers).** VERIFIED via the Allahabad HC order quoting SOP para 10.2.
- **SOP date.** Unclear: LiveLaw opinion says 2 Jan 2026; BS reports approval on 15 Jan 2026; the MP HC order cites an SOP
  dated 10.04.2026. The reel says "2026" only.
