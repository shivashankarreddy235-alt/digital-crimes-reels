"WhatsApp calls can't be traced"? Wrong.
⚖️ For educational purposes only. Not legal advice.

A WhatsApp call is end-to-end encrypted, so no one outside the chat can listen. But it travels as internet data that carries an IP address, a port and a time. Under DoT's licence rules, operators keep these IP Detail Records (IPDR) for at least two years. On a lawful order (BNSS Section 94; IT Rules 2021, rule 3(1)(j)), investigators match the app's IP logs with the operator's records, and prove them in court with a BSA Section 63 certificate. Extortion by threats: BNS Section 308.

Got a threat call? Don't pay and don't delete anything: screenshot the number, date and time, and report at cybercrime.gov.in or to the police.
