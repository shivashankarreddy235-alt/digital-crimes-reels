Pending challan on WhatsApp? Don't tap it.
⚖️ For educational purposes only. Not legal advice.

The "RTO e-Challan" file is not a challan. It is an Android app (APK) that can read your SMS, forward your OTPs to fraudsters and, as reported, send itself on to your contacts. That is why it often comes from a friend. Pretending to be the traffic police to cheat is an offence under IT Act Section 66D; Section 66C (identity theft) and BNS Sections 318(4) and 319 can also apply.

Check challans only on echallan.parivahan.gov.in or the mParivahan app. Already tapped it? Call 1930 and your bank at once, and report on cybercrime.gov.in.
