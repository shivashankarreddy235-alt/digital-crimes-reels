# EP08 — Pending challan on WhatsApp? Don't tap it

**Series:** SCAM ALERT (red badge) · **Primary audience:** vehicle owners and everyday WhatsApp / UPI users (second audience:
parents and families, who receive these files in family groups). Send line: "Send this to someone who drives."
Slot: 2026-11-26 pm (8:00 PM IST) · on-screen text English · voice **Hindi** (AI voice: the ElevenLabs voice set in the owner's
account) · section numbers and 1930 written as English words so they are spoken in English (owner's EP03 rule).
Format: **35–45 s** (strategy doc, "Reach fixes", 25 Nov 2026 — the first reel built in the short format). Frame 1 is the hook;
chapter labels are small top-corner labels with a sting (no full-screen chapter cards); at most one law card; other sections in the
caption; the checklist holds for the screenshot before a 3-second outro.
Why this topic today: police in Mangaluru, Chennai and Hyderabad registered or probed fake e-challan cases between 20 and 24 Nov, and
this week's Mangaluru case came from a friend's WhatsApp (see trends.md).
Scenario: **dramatised** — "Neha, 32, Pune", the friend "Rohan", the chat, the file size, the accounts and the amounts (₹38,500 +
₹21,000, marked "Illustrative amounts") are invented and masked. No real person, case, account or client.
Explain, never enable: the reel says what the fake app does (reads SMS, forwards OTPs, can spread to contacts) and how to stay safe.
Nothing on how such apps are built, hidden or spread.
Voiceover: 101 words (Hindi), 8 segments. Planned length about 42 s (final length set by the voice).

Five-part spine: 01 THE SCAM (scene 2) · 02 HOW IT WORKS (3) · 03 THE TRAIL (4) · 04 THE LAW (5) · 05 DO THIS NOW (6–7).
Scam Alert weighting: parts 1 and 5 get the most time (hook + scam ≈ 10 s; check + screenshot card ≈ 12 s).
