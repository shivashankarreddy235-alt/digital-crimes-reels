# Sources: EP08 "Pending challan on WhatsApp? Don't tap it" (SCAM ALERT)

Morning build, 26 Nov 2026. Every page below was opened by this run unless marked otherwise. Quotes are short; the rest is
paraphrase. Explain, never enable: the reel says what the fake "challan" app does (concept level) and how to stay safe. It gives no
detail of how such an app is built, hidden or distributed. Every name, number, amount and file size on screen is invented and masked.

## The scam and how the fake "challan" app works

- **Fake traffic challans arrive by SMS and WhatsApp, say a fine is pending, push immediate payment, and carry links, APK files or
  PDFs; once clicked or downloaded, attackers may reach banking data and OTPs.** VERIFIED.
  - PIB Fact Check post of 9 Jul 2026, reported by Business Standard, 10 Jul 2026: "Clicking such links can compromise your OTPs"
    and lead to "unauthorised bank/UPI transactions". Advice: don't click links from SMS or WhatsApp, avoid APKs, check challans only
    on the official e-Challan portal, report on 1930 / cybercrime.gov.in.
    https://www.business-standard.com/india-news/fake-traffic-challan-scam-pib-warning-whatsapp-sms-links-126071000400_1.html
  - DoT / Sanchar Saathi advisory sheet "Fake Traffic Challan" (undated): "Traffic Police NEVER send direct payment links via SMS,
    WhatsApp, or Email"; "Do Not Download APKs and click on suspicious links". Official places named: mParivahan
    (https://mparivahan.parivahan.gov.in), https://echallan.parivahan.gov.in, Sanchar Saathi, cybercrime.gov.in / 1930.
    https://sancharsaathi.gov.in/SancharSaathiDocuments/FakeTrafficChallan/FakeTrafficChallan.pdf
- **The "challan" is really an Android app (APK). Once installed it asks for SMS, contacts and call permissions and to become the
  default SMS app, then forwards incoming SMS (OTPs) to the fraudsters.** VERIFIED.
  - The Quint WebQoof, 2 May 2025: the message asks the user to install "NextGen mParivahan"; the APK "contains malware", asks for
    contacts, SMS and call permissions and to be the default messaging app, and forwards received SMS to the fraudsters, which lets
    them get OTPs for banking and shopping apps. "Do not install any applications apart from those from the Play Store."
    https://www.thequint.com/news/webqoof/e-challan-scam-where-victims-are-targeted-with-malware-via-apk-files
  - CloudSEK research reported by IANS / Siasat, 17 Jul 2024 (old, context only): fake e-challan APKs (Wromba family) requested
    default-SMS status, intercepted OTPs, extracted contacts; 4,400+ devices infected.
    https://www.siasat.com/vietnamese-hackers-fuelling-whatsapp-e-challan-scam-in-india-report-3063220/
- **It often arrives from a known contact; the app is reported to forward the same message to the victim's contacts.** VERIFIED
  as reported (worded "can" / "reported" on screen and in the voice).
  - Digit, 4 Nov 2025: the APK is sent over WhatsApp "often from a known contact"; reportedly it "automatically forwards the same
    scam message to all contacts", and some users' WhatsApp numbers were temporarily banned after it spammed their contacts.
    https://www.digit.in/news/apps/whatsapp-users-beware-fake-rto-challan-apk-scam-spreading-fast-can-steal-your-data-and-get-your-number-banned.html
  - Vartha Bharati, 24 Nov 2026: in Mangaluru an "RTO e-Challan" APK arrived on WhatsApp apparently from a friend's account;
    "Believing that the message had genuinely been sent by his friend, he clicked on the file"; two withdrawals of ₹50,000 followed;
    Mangaluru CEN police registered a case. (Used for "Why this topic today" only; the reel's scenario is dramatised and different.)
    https://english.varthabharati.in/karavali/youngster-loses-rs-1-lakh-after-downloading-fake-rto-e-challan-apk-in-mangaluru-2293412

## The law (one law card on screen; the rest in the caption)

  to ₹1 lakh.** VERIFIED (bare text). "Whoever, by means of any communication device or computer resource cheats by personation,
  shall be punished with imprisonment of either description for a term which may extend to three years and shall also be liable to
  fine which may extend to one lakh rupees." UNODC SHERLOC:
  https://sherloc.unodc.org/cld/fr/legislation/ind/the_information_technology_act_2000/chapter_xi/sections_66c66d/sections_66c-66d.html
  - Fits the message that pretends to come from the traffic police / RTO. Applied (with 66C) in the reported Udupi case.
- **IT Act 2000 s.66C — identity theft: fraudulent or dishonest use of another person's electronic signature, password or other
  unique identification feature: up to 3 years and fine up to ₹1 lakh.** VERIFIED (bare text, same SHERLOC page).
  - Caption only (using the victim's OTPs and banking login).
- **IT Act 2000 s.43(a)–(c) — access without permission, copying data, introducing a computer contaminant or virus; liability to pay
  compensation.** VERIFIED (bare text): https://incometaxindia.gov.in/Acts/Information%20Technology%20Act,%202000/102120000000005390.htm

## The one-minute phone check

- **Android's "Install unknown apps" permission is granted per app (e.g. a browser or messaging app) and can be switched off in
  Settings.** VERIFIED.
  - Android Developers Blog, 22 Aug 2017 ("Making it safer to get apps on Android O"): "This permission is tied to the app that
    prompts the install"; "The Settings app shows the list of apps that the user has approved for installing unknown apps."
    https://android-developers.googleblog.com/2017/08/making-it-safer-to-get-apps-on-android-o.html
  - Samsung support (updated 22 Sep 2026): Settings > Security and privacy > More security settings > Install unknown apps, then
    each app's switch. https://www.samsung.com/ae/support/mobile-devices/how-to-enable-permission-to-install-apps-from-unknown-source-on-my-samsung-phone
  - Menus differ by phone, so the reel says: open Settings, search "Install unknown apps", keep WhatsApp (and your browser) OFF.
