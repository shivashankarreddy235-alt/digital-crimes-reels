Your phone is seized. Who proves nothing on it changed?
⚖️ For educational purposes only. Not legal advice.

A seizure must be video-recorded (BNSS Section 105), and experts usually examine a forensic copy of the phone's data. That copy gets a hash value: a 64-character code that changes if even one character changes. Under BSA Section 63(4), the evidence certificate names the device (make, model, IMEI) and records this hash, with Part B signed by an expert; in May 2026 the Supreme Court upheld the rule, calling a hash value an "electronic fingerprint".

Note your phone's IMEI today (Settings, or the code in the reel). If your phone is ever seized, ask a lawyer to compare the hash values.
