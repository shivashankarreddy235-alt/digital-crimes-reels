Companies must report cyber incidents, ransomware included, to CERT-In within 6 hours of noticing them (CERT-In Directions of April 2022, IT Act Section 70B(6)). Ignoring the directions can mean up to 1 year in jail or a fine up to ₹1 crore (Section 70B(7)). The ransom demand is extortion under BNS Section 308; the break-in falls under IT Act Section 66 read with Section 43. From 13 May 2027, DPDP Rules (rule 7) will also require telling affected people and the Data Protection Board.

Isolate systems, don't wipe them, report to CERT-In (toll-free 1800-11-4949) and complain at cybercrime.gov.in. Paying doesn't guarantee your files back.
