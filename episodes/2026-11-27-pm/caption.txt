Police arrested you? Know your rights.
⚖️ For educational purposes only. Not legal advice.

Grounds of arrest in writing, in your language (Art. 22(1), BNSS s.47) · bail information in a bailable offence (s.47(2)) · officer's name and an arrest memo signed by a witness and you (s.36) · family told (s.48) · meet your lawyer (s.38) · free legal aid (LSA Act s.12) · medical examination (s.53) · Magistrate within 24 hours (Art. 22(2), s.58) · not compelled to be a witness against yourself (Art. 20(3)) · women: no arrest between sunset and sunrise without a Magistrate's prior permission (s.43(5)).

Supreme Court, 5 Nov 2026: the 24 hours run from when you are actually held. Free legal aid: 15100 (NALSA).
