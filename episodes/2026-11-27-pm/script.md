Accuracy notes: the bail right is limited to bailable offences (s.47(2)); the lawyer may be met during questioning, not
throughout it (s.38); the night-arrest bar has an exception (exceptional circumstances, woman officer, prior permission of
the Judicial Magistrate, s.43(5)) and is shown with it; "not forced to confess / be a witness against yourself" follows
Art. 20(3) and is not presented as a general right to refuse all questions.
