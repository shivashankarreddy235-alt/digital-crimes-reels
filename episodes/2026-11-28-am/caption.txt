Arrested? Your next 24 hours.
⚖️ For educational purposes only. Not legal advice.

Hour 0 is when police actually hold you, not when the arrest is written down (Art. 22(2), BNSS s.58; Supreme Court, Sujit Das, 5 Nov 2026). Then: an arrest memo (s.36), the grounds of arrest (s.47), family told where you are (s.48), a medical check (s.53), your lawyer during questioning (s.38), bail at the station in a bailable offence (s.47(2), s.478), and written grounds in your language at least 2 hours before remand (Mihir Rajesh Shah, SC, 2025). Within 24 hours, travel time excluded, a Magistrate decides: release, bail or custody (s.187).

In custody? Free legal aid is your right (LSA Act s.12): NALSA helpline 15100.
