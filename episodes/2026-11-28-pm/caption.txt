Named in a cyber case? Arrest isn't automatic.
⚖️ For educational purposes only. Not legal advice.

For offences up to 7 years, a notice to appear is the rule and arrest the exception, with reasons in writing (BNSS s.35; Supreme Court, Satender Kumar Antil, 15 Jan 2026). The notice must be served by hand, not on WhatsApp, and you must comply. IT Act 3-year offences like s.66, 66C and 66D are bailable (s.77B), but BNS cheating, s.318(4), is non-bailable. An Inspector or above investigates IT Act offences (s.78). A seized phone: seizure list, video-recorded seizure (BNSS s.105), hash value (BSA s.63).

A "police" video call saying you're under arrest is a scam: call 1930 or report on cybercrime.gov.in.
