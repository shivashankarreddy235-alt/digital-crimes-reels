## Decision
Keep the planned series and audience (Myth vs Fact; everyone, parents). The 35–45 s format and the one-idea rule leave room
for 3 myths, not 5, so the reel is built as one idea — "the money myths that cost you, and why reporting fast gets money
back": (1) you enter a PIN to receive money (NPCI: never); (2) the money is gone, so don't report (1930 / the portal can
block it in the next accounts; MHA: ₹11,158 crore+ saved; this week's ₹1.04 lakh refund in Delhi); (3) the bank never pays
back (RBI's zero-liability rule today, and RBI's new rules from 1 Jan 2027). Dropped from the plan: "police arrest on video
calls" (EP12, 28 Nov, and EP03), "receiving money is harmless" (EP05's account-freeze reel), "deleted means gone" (planned
for EP27's privacy myths), "crypto is untraceable" (better as a Forensics File; back to the topic bank).
